What does the color mean?

The balance gap is (1 − α) × relative success gain − α × relative cost reduction. White is zero; color saturates at ±5 percentage points of relative change. Values are averaged across three seeds. A small gap indicates alignment with the requested direction, and can also occur when both gains are small.
